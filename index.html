    DUTCH REPUBLIC GUIDE

    Not sure what to buy? Start here.

    You don't need to know every strain, terpene, or product type to shop well. A few simple ideas can make your experience way better.

    What are terpenes?

    They are the natural aromatic compounds responsible for the smell and flavor of a strain, but they also help influence how that product tends to feel. It's why two products with similar THC percentages can create very different experiences.

    • Myrcene: Relaxing & Calm
    • Limonene: Bright & Uplifting
    • Linalool: Soothing & Grounding

    What is my dose?

    There is no perfect number that works for everyone. Tolerance, mood, environment, food, and body chemistry all matter. Start small. You can always take more. You can never take less.

    Edible Timing: Most take 30–90 minutes to begin, peak around 2–3 hours, and can last 4–8 hours.
